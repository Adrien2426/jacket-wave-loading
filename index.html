Yellow brace: two force contributions, three coordinate components

Separate contributions following the lecture-style approach, then resolved using the brace geometry. These are illustrative signed forces in kN, not calculated wave loads or separate maxima. Use contributions at the same instant.

Blue: Fₓ = Fₙ⁽ᵘ⁾. The force from u is already along x.
Purple: Fₙ⁽ʷ⁾ acts normal to the brace in the y–z plane.
Green: Fᵧ = Fₙ⁽ʷ⁾ sin α
Orange: F𝓏 = Fₙ⁽ʷ⁾ cos α
Teal: complete normal force vector Fₙ = (Fₓ, Fᵧ, F𝓏).

Green + orange = purple. Blue + purple = teal. Do not add the components again to their parent vectors. The orange arrow is shifted head-to-tail to show the decomposition. All force arrows use one display scale. Reverse either slider to see the force directions reverse.

The original vertical velocity is w. Its normal part is w⊥ = (0, w sin α cos α, w cos² α). The remaining parallel part completes the original velocity.
